Smart Paste · ⌥V

Paste it in the shape you need.

Copy something, press ⌥V on your Mac, and Smart Paste shows only the actions that fit what you copied, with a preview. Choose one, and the result pastes straight into the app you’re using.

  1. 1Copy a link, some text, a list, JSON or screenshots.
  2. 2Press ⌥V where you want to paste.
  3. 3Pick an action. The preview shows the result before it pastes.

Free and Pro

What Smart Paste can do.

Free

  • Links. Clean URL, Markdown Link, URL Encode and Decode, Open URL, and Generate GET as cURL, fetch, Python, Swift or raw HTTP.
  • Lines. Trim, Trim Lines, Sort Lines, Dedupe Lines and Paste Plain.
  • Case & naming. UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case.
  • Fix Keyboard Layout. Typed with the wrong layout? Paste it in the right one, with compatible layouts such as Hebrew, Russian, French and German.
  • Recent images. Paste the latest screenshot, or up to 3 combined or separately.

Cliptika Pro

  • JSON. JSON Pretty and JSON Minify.
  • Translate. Into the language set in Settings, with a preview first. macOS 15 or later.
  • Images. Resize and convert: Resize 50%, Max 1024px, Max 512px, As JPEG and As PNG.
