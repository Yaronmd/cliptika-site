Cliptika Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02 · Applies to Cliptika 0.4.0 for macOS

Cliptika does not automatically send your clipboard content or screenshots to us. The app has no account system, analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs, and its core features work locally on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

To provide its features, Cliptika stores clipboard history (text, links, images, and file references), Pinned items, Projects, notes, Workflows, screenshots you take, and your settings. This data is kept in the app's storage on your Mac and is not sent anywhere. You can delete items inside the app. Removing the app alone may leave local support files and saved data behind; contact us if you need help locating and removing them.

Permissions

  • Screen Recording enables screenshot and on-screen text capture when you use those features.
  • Accessibility enables direct paste into another app and, if you grant access, can read the active document path exposed by supported editors such as Xcode and VS Code to provide project context.
  • Automation lets Cliptika read the Finder window and selection, Xcode workspace, or Terminal tab information to determine your current file, folder, or project.

All are optional. You can change or revoke them at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Sensitive content

Clipboard history can contain sensitive information. Review and clear stored items in the app whenever you wish.

Support and diagnostics

If you choose Send Diagnostics, Cliptika copies a technical status report to your clipboard and opens your mail app. It does not send the report automatically; you decide whether to paste and email it to us. We use any information you choose to send for support.

Children

Cliptika is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
