Extract Text Pro

Copy text you can’t select.

Text in an image, a slide or a screenshot can’t be highlighted or copied. Extract Text reads the area you choose — on your Mac — and puts the words on your clipboard.

  1. Open it from Tools in the menu bar, or search for it in the Command Palette (⌥Space).
  2. Drag over the text you want.
  3. Cliptika reads it on-device and copies it: “Text copied”.
  4. Paste it anywhere, and edit it. It’s in Clipboard History too.

Cliptika reads what is visible in the area you select. If nothing readable is found, it says “No text found” and leaves your clipboard alone.

Command Palette · ⌥Space

Every action, one search away.

Press ⌥Space, type what you want to do, and run it. Smart Paste and Lens answer what you just copied; the Command Palette is for everything else.

  • Search by name: “uuid”, “color”, “screenshot”, “clean url”
  • Works alongside Smart Paste (⌥V) and Lens (⌥A)

Pick Color · ⌥⇧C

Sample any color on screen.

Pick Color opens the native loupe. Click a pixel and Cliptika copies its HEX value. It lands in Clipboard History, so you can pin it or file it in a Collection.

  • Free
  • Also in Tools and the Command Palette

Capture · ⌥⇧S · ⌥⇧F

Capture it. Paste it. Keep moving.

Drag over an area and the capture goes straight to your clipboard, ready to paste into a message or document.

  • Select an area (⌥⇧S) Pro
  • Full screen (⌥⇧F) is free

Cliptika Lens · ⌥A

Actions for what you just copied.

Copy a file or folder, then press ⌥A. Lens offers Copy Path, Reveal, New File and Copy Folder Path, plus Quick Insert for dates, times and recent clipboard items.

  • Open Terminal Here, UUID and Unix timestamp are Pro
  • Everything else in Lens is free
