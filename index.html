Product

Everything within reach.

Five focused ways to make the things you copy, capture, and reuse work harder.

Clipboard History

Everything you copy, right where you left it.

Search and reuse text, links, images, and files in seconds — without digging back through old windows or chats.

  • Fast, focused history search
  • Keep more than plain text close

Smart Paste · ⌥V

Copy. Paste. Done — smarter.

Press ⌥V and Smart Paste offers the actions that fit what you just copied, then pastes the result where you are working.

  • Transform text: UPPERCASE, Title Case, Remove Duplicate Lines and more
  • Translate copied text in place

Pins & Collections

Keep what matters, organized your way.

Pin the clips you return to, and group saved clips in Collections with your own order, icon and color.

  • Free includes 3 Collections with up to 25 items each
  • Pins and Collection items are never removed by automatic History cleanup

Cliptika Lens

Act on what you have, right now.

Open Lens after copying a file or folder, or when working in a supported editor, to reach relevant actions from the keyboard.

  • Copy a file or folder, then open Lens
  • Quick Insert for dates, times, and recent items

Capture & OCR

Capture it. Paste it. Keep moving.

Capture exactly what you need, then paste the result into the conversation or app in front of you.

  • Selected area, ready to send
  • Real captured content, ready to reuse

Made for a quieter Mac.

Keyboard-first

Reach common actions with a shortcut, then get back to what you were doing.

Local-first

Your clipboard history stays on your Mac. No account required.

Native by design

A focused macOS utility, not another browser-shaped workspace.
