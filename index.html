Real tasks, real shortcuts

Made for the way you work.

Pick what sounds like you. Every example below is something Cliptika 0.4 does today.

  • Get to the file, fast

    Copy a file or folder, press ⌥A, and Lens offers Copy Path, Reveal, New File, Copy Folder Path and Open Terminal Here Pro.

  • Clean up what you paste

    Pretty-print or minify copied JSON Pro, clean a tracking URL, encode or decode it, or generate a ready-to-run GET request in cURL or Swift.

  • Never retype a command

    Search your clipboard history for that snippet from yesterday, or pin the commands and paths you reuse every day.

  • Grab exactly what you need

    Select an area of the screen and paste the result straight into your next message or document. Full-screen capture is included; area capture is part of Pro.

  • Sample any color

    Pick Color opens the native loupe and copies the HEX value of any pixel you can see.

  • Keep references together

    Pin brand colors and boilerplate, and group visual references in Collections with your own name, icon and color.

  • Collect sources and quotes

    Save quotes, notes and links into Collections. Free includes 3 Collections with up to 25 items each.

  • Copy text you can’t select

    Select an area of the screen and Cliptika copies the text it reads there Pro. Handy for images, slides and scanned pages.

  • Find it, even weeks later

    Search your history by what you remember, and read copied text in another language with in-place translation Pro (macOS 15 or later).

  • Reuse what you already wrote

    Find an address, a reply or a link you copied earlier, or pin a message you send again and again.

  • Tidy text as you paste

    Press ⌥V for Title Case, UPPERCASE, lowercase, Remove Duplicate Lines, Sort Lines or Trim Whitespace — pasted straight into the app you’re in.

  • Share cleaner links

    Clean URL strips tracking parameters from a copied link before you send it.

Pro marks actions that are part of Cliptika Pro. Everything else is included free.

In depth

A closer look.

Smart Paste · ⌥V

Copy. Paste. Done — smarter.

Press ⌥V and Smart Paste offers the actions that fit what you just copied, then pastes the result where you are working.

  • Text: UPPERCASE, Title Case, Remove Duplicate Lines and more
  • Links, JSON and images get their own actions
  • Translate copied text in place

Pins & Collections

Keep what matters, organized your way.

Pin the clips you return to, and group saved clips in Collections with your own order, icon and color.

  • Free includes 3 Collections with up to 25 items each
  • Pins and Collection items are never removed by automatic History cleanup

Cliptika Lens · ⌥A

Act on what you have, right now.

Copy a file or folder, then open Lens to reach the actions that apply to it.

  • Copy Path, Reveal, New File, Copy Folder Path
  • Quick Insert for dates, times and recent clipboard items

Capture

Capture it. Paste it. Keep moving.

Select exactly what you need, then paste the result into the conversation or app in front of you.

  • Selected area, ready to send
  • Pick Color and Extract Text are a Command Palette search away
