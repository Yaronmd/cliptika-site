What Cliptika does

  • Find anything you copied

    Text, links, images and files stay in your history. Search, and it’s back in a second.

  • Paste smarter, without extra steps

    Press ⌥V to paste a clean link, a tidy list or the right letter case, straight in.

  • Capture and organize what you need

    Turn several screenshots into one image, and keep what you reuse in Pins and Collections.

See it in action

One shortcut. The right paste.

Smart Paste

⌥V

Copied a link full of tracking? Press ⌥V on your Mac and choose Clean URL. Only the link you meant gets pasted.

Interactive Demo Sample content only. It never reads or changes your clipboard. Tap Replay, or tap an action to try it.

There’s more
