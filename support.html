Getting started

Cliptika lives in your menu bar. Click its icon to search your clipboard, open Collections and Pinned items, and use Quick Actions. Discover Cliptika, in the main window, shows what it can do.

Shortcuts

Smart Paste opens with ⌥V and Cliptika Lens with ⌥A by default. Review and change shortcuts in Settings › Shortcuts; Lens options are in Settings › Cliptika Lens.

Free and Pro

Clipboard History, Smart Paste for text, lines and links, Fix Keyboard Layout, Collections and Capture Stack (up to 3 images) are free. Cliptika Pro adds Extract Text, Translate (macOS 15 or later), JSON tools, image resizing and conversion, custom history retention, unlimited Pins and Collections, Capture Stacks of up to 10 images, and power tools. See Free & Pro.

Screenshots or Extract Text don't work

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording (on macOS 15 or later: Screen & System Audio Recording) and turn Cliptika on. After you enable it, quit and reopen Cliptika if macOS asks you to, then try again. Settings › Permissions & Privacy in Cliptika shows the current status.

Pasting into other apps doesn't work

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and turn Cliptika on. If it already shows as on but pasting still fails, turn it off and on again, or quit and reopen Cliptika. If you are asked to allow access to a specific file or folder, use the macOS file dialog Cliptika shows.

Where is my data?

Cliptika keeps its clipboard history, Collections, screenshots and settings locally on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy for details, and contact us if you need help removing stored data.

Reporting a problem

Email us with your macOS version, your Cliptika version and what you were doing. Settings › Diagnostics & Support can copy a status report for you to paste into the email; it never includes clipboard content or file paths.
