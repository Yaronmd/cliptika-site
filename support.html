Getting started

Cliptika lives in your menu bar. You can open Discover Cliptika from the main window at any time to see what it can do.

Shortcuts

Smart Paste opens with ⌥V by default. Cliptika Lens opens with ⌥A by default, giving you relevant actions for your current context and Quick Insert tools. Review your shortcuts and available customization in Settings › Shortcuts. Lens-specific options are available in Settings › Cliptika Lens.

A permission is not working

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and enable Cliptika under Screen Recording, Accessibility, and Automation as needed. If a permission shows as enabled but does not work, quit and reopen Cliptika.

Where is my data?

Cliptika keeps its clipboard history and settings locally on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy for details and contact us if you need help removing stored data.

Reporting a problem

Email us with your macOS version, the Cliptika version (0.4.0), and what you were doing. Settings › Diagnostics & Support shows app status you can include.
