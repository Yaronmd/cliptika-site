material only. The review/reference transcodes in `assets/video/manual-candidates/` are also
retained but are not referenced by the homepage.

encoded directly from its approved high-resolution MOV source on 2026-10-09; no original MOV was
modified. The production clips are framed in the source encode rather than enlarged with CSS.
